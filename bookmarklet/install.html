QRick bookmarklet

Generate and scan QR codes. Everything stays on your device.

Drag the button onto your bookmarks bar. Press it on a page to generate a QR code, or choose Scan to read one using your camera. Camera scanning requires an HTTPS page that allows camera access. Scanning uses only the browser’s native QR detection API. If your browser does not support it, use the QRick extension to scan; QR generation still works in the bookmarklet.
